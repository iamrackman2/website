The label

About

Distants Records is an underground progressive label from Buenos Aires, Argentina. Since 2009 we have released dark, deep progressive house from Argentina and around the world, built release by release, by and for the people who play it.

Supported by Paul Oakenfold, Markus Schulz, Anthony Pappa, Moshic, Lemon8, Ingo Vogelmann, Lacandon & VeNes, dPen, Henry Saiz, Michael & Levan, Chris Drifter, Ozgur Ozkan, Taucher, Fernando Ferreyra, BS AS Deep, Frangellico.

Releases on Bandcamp, Beatport and all major stores and streaming platforms.
